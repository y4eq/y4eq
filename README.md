### About Me

I'm a Brazilian vibe coder focused on turning ideas into working interfaces and software quickly, without overcomplicating things. I build websites and desktop applications using **HTML**, **CSS**, **JavaScript**, **TypeScript**, **Node.js**, and **Electron**.

My work spans from practical utilities that simplify daily workflows and make access to resources faster, to purely experimental or oddly specific tools that are sometimes useless on paper yet genuinely interesting to build and use.

<br>

### What I Focus On

- **Vibe Coding & Rapid Prototyping** — Moving fast from raw concept to functional UI and interactive tools, refining ideas by building directly.
- **Web & Electron Apps** — Creating clean web experiences and lightweight desktop applications with **Node.js** and **Electron**.
- **Resource Access & Utilities** — Building tools, launchers, and helpers designed to make accessing features, workflows, and resources more direct.
- **Useful & Weirdly Interesting Tools** — Mixing practical everyday utilities with niche, experimental, or unconventional ideas that spark curiosity.
